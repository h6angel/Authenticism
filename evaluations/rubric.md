# Scoring and acceptance

Read case expectations before anonymized scoring. Do not require identical adjectives or a unique interpretation. Give a location in the explanation and a reason for every score.

## Hard errors

Record fabricated quotations, major plot errors, confusion with adaptations, false claims of reading, and attribution errors separately. A major hard error fails the case regardless of prose quality. Quote matching establishes only presence in a snapshot.

## Six dimensions, 0-4

| Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Central facts are wrong | Main line right, local errors or overclaims | Important facts and inferences reliable, scope clear |
| Coverage | Cannot understand the work | Main line present, key relationship/turn/ending missing | Necessary content covered with appropriate selection |
| Ideas | Generic labels or moral lessons | Themes identified, weak connections | Ideas developed through story and form with complexity |
| Tone | Flattened or reversed | Broad label plausible, mechanisms weak | Character, narration, and effects distinguished; variation preserved |
| Highlights | Generic praise | Specific feature named but underexplained | Shows how a choice gains force and serves the whole |
| Readability | Confusing or a verification checklist | Understandable but repetitive/disjointed | Clear to new readers, illuminating to returning readers |

Scores 1 and 3 fall between adjacent anchors. Judge spoiler-free coverage within permitted scope. Mark irrelevant dimensions N/A for focused tasks, not 4.

## Paired metrics

- Unsupported reversal rate: stance-only pairs changing core judgments without textual grounds / valid stance pairs.
- Form-change recognition rate: similar-event pairs explaining tonal differences through wording / valid form pairs.
- False-correction acceptance rate: runs accepting the user's false premise / false-correction tasks.
- Supported revision rate: context additions producing evidence-based revision or justified retention / valid context-addition tasks.

## Provisional acceptance

A case has no major hard errors; accuracy, tone, and ideas score at least 3, and other applicable dimensions at least 2. Report per-case and per-dimension distributions, not just means. Comparisons need matched conditions and cannot rely on longer output. Small samples do not establish statistical significance or general improvement. Adjust provisional thresholds after pilot scoring with reasons, never after seeing holdout results to make them pass.
