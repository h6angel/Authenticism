# Runtime and reading simplification check

Date: 2026-11-16. Exploratory development comparison, not a general literary benchmark.

## Change and execution

Ordinary chat no longer requires a research archive. A requested essay file alone does not trigger one; requested archives, evaluations, and direct-quotation verification retain their relevant records and checks. Reading guidance was reduced from 1,942 to 680 whitespace-separated English words, retaining action, tone, form, evidence boundaries, and interpretive revision. No scripts, schema, agent metadata, or new writing constraints were added.

Six fresh subagents answered three Chinese requests under before/after instruction snapshots: a complete introduction to The Gift of the Magi, a complete explanation of The Yellow Wallpaper, and a self-contained focused follow-up on Gregor's concern about lateness in The Metamorphosis. Each condition ran once with inherited model/settings and tools, no imposed answer length. Executors did not receive fixes, expectations, or earlier answers. This compares instruction versions, not skill versus no skill. See [run manifest](run-manifest.json), [snapshot hashes](snapshot.json), and [instruction diff](instructions.patch).

## Source coverage and observable behavior

Retrospective hosted by Cambridge before and American Yawp after. |
| Metamorphosis follow-up | Same English translation opening, lines 30–117 | Both stay focused on work, family debt, internalized judgment, and comic mismatch. Neither retells the whole novella. |

These are semantic observations, not proof that every added paragraph improves quality. One sample per condition cannot separate instruction effects from sampling variation or retrieval differences. The follow-up is supplied as a self-contained question, not a full multi-turn test. Whole-novel performance remains untested.

## Blinded review result

An independent reviewer received shuffled A/B pairs, the rubric, and source leads, without instruction versions, proposed changes, run metadata, or the mapping. It opened the relevant primary texts and the author's retrospective. Its unchanged [review](blind-review.md), [decoded scores](scores.json), and [mapping](blind-mapping.json) are retained.

| Pair | Before: accuracy / coverage / ideas / tone / highlights / readability | After | Reviewer judgment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 | 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 | Before slightly more compact; after develops additional details but repeats value judgments. |
| Full explanation | 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 | 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 | Tie; both develop scenes, with mild repetition. |
| Focused follow-up | 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 | 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 4 | Before slightly fuller on self-surveillance; after remains complete and focused. |

All six provisionally passed with no major hard error. These high single-rater scores have a ceiling effect and do not establish broad quality. The results support retaining the shorter instructions as a workable development revision, not claiming better prose. Minor before-version advantages are preserved rather than averaged away. No further writing rule was added on the basis of these isolated differences.

## Record overhead and harness contamination

Before-version introduction and explanation produced research records; revised counterparts did not. However, the revised follow-up also generated an archive. Its executor confirmed that the harness phrase “For evaluator capture” activated evaluation-record instructions. The earlier follow-up produced none. Consequently, these counts do not establish ordinary-chat overhead or a reliable speed improvement. Approximate elapsed times are executor self-reports and are not controlled timing measurements.

A separate executor received a plain article-export request without evaluation/capture wording. It created only [answer.md](ordinary-file/answer.md); directory inspection found no research archive. This supports the intended article-export behavior for that run, not a universal guarantee. The executor's explanation of the contaminated run is retained in [audit](harness-audit.md).

## Evidence and limitations

Answers and execution coverage are preserved under runs/. Files generated by executors are retained without rewriting. The three generated research-note files pass structural checks; quote checks report zero registered direct quotations and do not validate essay interpretation. See [record checks](record-checks.json).

This is a development check with one independent blinded model reviewer, not a human-scored or repeated holdout study. The author already knows the development cases; no unused synthetic holdout is consumed. Source/tool reports are self-reported summaries rather than complete tool transcripts. Exact model deployment and numeric token budgets were not exposed. Existing code was unchanged, so its unit suite was not rerun; fixture validation, quote fixture matching, skill validation, relative-document-link checks, and diff whitespace checks passed.
