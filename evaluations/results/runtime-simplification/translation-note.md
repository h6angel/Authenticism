# English Publication and Historical Provenance

The comparison was executed on November 16, 2026 with Chinese requests and answers. For an English-only repository, those requests, answers, the blind review, and Chinese descriptive fields in execution and research records have been translated into English. These are publication translations, not new executions or new reviews. No score or before/after mapping has been changed.

The translated answers retain the original paragraph sequence, claims, qualifications, and source links. Review paragraph numbers refer to the answer body, excluding the publication notice. Translation changes wording and may affect perceived prose quality; the historical readability scores apply to the original Chinese answers, not to these English versions. An English-language performance claim requires fresh runs and review.

The original Chinese records have been copied to the Git-ignored local directory `evaluations/local-runs/pre-english-translation/runtime-simplification/`. That local backup is not distributed with the repository. Where the original files were already committed, Git history retains them; uncommitted originals are only in the local backup until separately preserved.

`snapshot.json`, `instructions.patch`, execution metadata, `record-checks.json`, and scores describe the historical run and its checks. Snapshot hashes are historical identifiers, not hashes of the current instructions after subsequent edits. The old check report is not evidence that translated files were checked at the time. Source excerpts have not been translated or rewritten; they remain in their original source language. Original answers were not rerun merely to obtain English text.
