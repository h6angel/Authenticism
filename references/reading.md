# Develop an understanding of the work

Use these methods to turn accessible material into an understanding of story, people, tone, ideas, and form. They guide reading rather than prescribe article headings. See [research.md](research.md) for retrieval and [explanation.md](explanation.md) for writing.

## Establish development and coverage

Map the initial situation, consequential changes, relationships, and ending, distinguishing event order from narrative order. For works organized around consciousness, imagery, or voice, follow that movement. Read continuous scenes where actions or relationships change, patterns concentrate, or the ending becomes intelligible, including material that resists the first interpretation.

Keep actual coverage clear: primary passages support their events and treatment; reliable synopses can fill the story map but cannot establish unread wording or rhythm. Interviews and criticism offer interpretive leads, not substitutes for scenes. Attribute observations about translated wording to the edition read. Keyword search navigates text; neither frequency nor missing search hits establishes significance or absence.

Check whether the material supports development beyond a conclusion. Knowing an event happened may not explain how an interaction unfolds. If the opening is well covered but later sections have only summaries, fill consequential gaps or limit affected claims rather than padding them with abstract discussion. Return to research for conflicting events, unknown turns or endings, or judgments resting on vague memory.

## Read tone through treatment

Distinguish character emotion, narrative attitude, and possible reader response. Suffering does not entail compassionate narration, and cold narration need not cancel a desolate situation. Explain tone through observable treatment:

- Diction and metaphor: how people and bodies are named, and whose language this is.
- Syntax and rhythm: effects of pauses, repetitions, delay, and abrupt turns, supported by wording actually read.
- Perspective and distance: entry into consciousness, withdrawal, or interruption of self-explanation.
- Repetition, attention, and response: what changes across recurrence, how suffering is acknowledged, and where expectations are interrupted.

Connect treatment to its target, effect, and scope. A few excerpts establish local effects, not whole-book proportions. Test dominant tendencies against changes of voice and moments of tenderness or cruelty; let contrary material narrow or change the account.

Do not assume trauma produces growth, art redeems, or tenderness conceals control. When sexuality, violence, humiliation, or sordid detail matters, explain its presentation rather than abstracting it into a theme. Stronger adjectives do not establish closer reading.

## Understand action and relationships

At consequential turns, consider the situation, what someone wants from whom, relevant fear or dependence, their action, and the other's response. Follow the changed circumstances into the next choice. These are questions to investigate, not a mandatory sequence.

Separate stated motives, behavior-supported inference, and unresolved elements. Self-deception or impulse cannot fill arbitrary evidence gaps. For claims of inevitability, distinguish what a character can bear from what the text actually excludes; the outcome alone does not establish the only possible path. Explain clear causation without forcing closure on genuine uncertainty.

## Develop ideas through form

Identify tensions enacted by choices and relationships, rather than assigning theme labels. Trace how later events and the ending complicate them. Distinguish a character's opinion, authorial intention, criticism, and the analyst's interpretation; none automatically settles the others.

Choose artistic features whose operation can be shown: a scene, image, voice, structural choice, or narrative distance. Ask what ordinary synopsis would lose, then explain that particular treatment and its force. Awkward or repellent writing may matter as much as beauty; discomfort alone does not demonstrate depth.

For judgments carrying the explanation, examine material likely to challenge them. Compare real competing readings by what they explain and what they assume, without manufacturing alternatives for balance.

## Reconsider with evidence

When the user offers a correction, identify whether it concerns fact, interpretation, tone, or personal response. Verify facts and explain which detail changes which judgment. Respect personal responses without turning them into the only possible reading. Confidence, repeated assertions, and prior assistant claims do not replace support.

Retain concise source references and necessary limitations. Use [notes-format.md](notes-format.md) only when structured records are needed. Before drafting, ensure the main development and selected scenes support the intended explanation; review the finished prose using explanation.md.
